# Business Insights & Recommendations

**Dataset note:** These are computed observations from the project's synthetic demonstration dataset, not real-world market findings.

## KPI snapshot
- Total revenue: ₹174,021,024.84
- Total profit: ₹45,167,311.30
- Unique orders: 6,000
- Units sold: 18,089
- Overall profit margin: 25.96%

## Observations
- Highest-revenue category: **Technology** (₹120,798,569.72).
- Highest-revenue region: **South** (₹37,608,685.40).
- Highest-revenue channel: **Online** (₹81,006,852.39).
- Highest-revenue product in this generated sample: **Headphones** (₹25,622,647.22).
- Monthly revenue ranges from ₹3,122,693.73 to ₹6,580,505.43 in the generated sample.

## Recommendations to discuss
1. **Inventory planning:** Compare category and product sales over time before changing replenishment levels.
2. **Regional action:** Investigate low-revenue regions alongside order count, product availability and local demand; revenue alone does not explain the cause.
3. **Discount governance:** Track profit margin by discount band. A higher sales total can still produce weaker profit.
4. **Channel investment:** Compare revenue share, profit and customer acquisition costs before reallocating channel budgets.
5. **Monthly monitoring:** Review revenue and profit together so that growth is not mistaken for profitable growth.

## Limitations
- Synthetic data is useful for demonstrating the workflow but cannot validate actual retail strategy.
- No returns, shipping costs, taxes, customer-level history or inventory snapshots are included.
- Product-level patterns are generated for practice and should not be generalized.
